Loading Protection 1.0.3 for Minecraft 1.16.5 (Forge)

Protects players from damage and mob targeting after joining. Protection ends
when the player moves more than 0.1 blocks from their join position or the
configured timer expires. Looking around alone keeps protection active.

Install this JAR in the mods folder of both the server and connecting Forge
clients. In singleplayer, install it in your Forge client instance.
Requires Minecraft 1.16.5 and Forge 36.x.

Configuration: config/loadingprotection-common.toml
